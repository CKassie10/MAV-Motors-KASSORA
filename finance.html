FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What documents do I need for finance approval?

+

You'll need a valid ID, recent payslips (3 months), proof of address, and banking details. Self-employed clients require additional documentation. We'll guide you through the entire process.

How quickly can I get approved?

+

Most approvals happen within 24-48 hours. For pre-approved clients, it can be same-day. We work directly with banks to expedite the process without compromising on security.

Can I get finance if I have bad credit?

+

Yes! We work with specialist finance providers who understand credit challenges. While rates may be slightly higher, we focus on getting you approved at the best possible rate for your situation.

Do you offer trade-in options with finance?

+

Absolutely. Trade-in value can be used as your deposit, reducing the amount you need to finance. We'll provide a fair market valuation of your current vehicle.

What's included in the monthly payment?

+

Your monthly payment covers the principal, interest, and insurance. Some packages include gap insurance and mechanical warranty. We'll provide a full breakdown before you commit.

Can I settle early without penalties?

+

Yes! Most finance options allow early settlement without penalties. You can pay off your loan whenever you want and save on interest charges.
